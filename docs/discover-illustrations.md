# Discover illustrations

Six category-specific illustrations were generated for Camellia: a sunny window/tea (REST), movement space (BODY), skincare vanity (BEAUTY), sketchbook/music/snack (PLAY), reading/writing desk (LEARN), and a welcoming tea table (CONNECT).

Files live in public/actions/illustrations as960px-wide WebP, approximately800KB total. Browser images are lazy-loaded and decoded asynchronously; no external image host is required. Original generated PNGs remain in the generation workspace. No user records, video frames, private conversation, names or personal images were supplied to image generation.

Illustrations are decorative category scenes. They do not claim to depict a particular venue, product, real person, medical effect or available reservation. Action titles and descriptions convey the actual activity.
